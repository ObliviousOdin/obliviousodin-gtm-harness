# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge
We pledge to make participation in AetherGTM a harassment-free experience for everyone.

## Standards
- Be respectful and precise
- Critique ideas, not people
- No harassment, hate speech, or personal attacks
- No spam or deceptive GTM tactics promoted as best practice

## Enforcement
Report issues to the repository owner via GitHub. Maintainers may remove content or block contributors who violate this code.
